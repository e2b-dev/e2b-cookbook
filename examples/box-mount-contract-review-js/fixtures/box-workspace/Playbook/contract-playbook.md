# Harbor Clinics: Vendor Contract Playbook

Synthetic example for demonstrations. Not legal advice.

Harbor Clinics buys software services. Use this playbook for a first-pass review of vendor agreements before the legal team reviews them.

## Review standard

- Compare every clause below with the agreement.
- For each deviation, cite the agreement section and quote the relevant words.
- Do not assume terms that the agreement does not contain. If a required term is missing, report it as missing.
- Assign a severity: **Critical** (do not sign), **High** (must negotiate), **Medium** (negotiate if possible), **Low** (note only).

## Approved positions

| Topic | Approved position | Severity if not met |
|---|---|---|
| Limitation of liability | Mutual cap, at least 12 months of fees. Carve-outs for confidentiality, data protection and indemnity. | Critical |
| Customer data use | Vendor uses customer data only to provide, secure and support the service. No model training on customer data without written opt-in. | Critical |
| Security incidents | Vendor notifies Harbor Clinics within 72 hours of discovering an incident that affects customer data. | High |
| Indemnity | Vendor indemnifies for third-party IP claims and for data breaches the vendor causes. | High |
| Termination | Either party can terminate for convenience with 30 days notice. Prepaid fees for the remaining term are refunded. | Medium |
| Renewal | Renewal terms of 12 months or less. Harbor Clinics can opt out with 30 days notice. | Medium |
| Service levels | 99.9% monthly uptime, with service credits. | Medium |
| Subprocessors | Vendor gives 30 days notice of new subprocessors. Harbor Clinics can object. | Low |
| Data return and deletion | Vendor returns data within 30 days of termination, then deletes it and confirms in writing. | Medium |

## Required output

1. **Summary**: two or three sentences and a recommendation (sign, negotiate, or do not sign).
2. **Deviations table**: Severity, Section, Agreement text, Approved position, Proposed change.
3. **Missing terms**: required positions that the agreement does not address.
4. **Next steps** for the legal team.
